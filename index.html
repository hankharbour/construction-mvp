Quantity assumptions

Wall area is rectangular perimeter × height, with no deductions for openings. Roof is a simple symmetric gable, with no overhangs, valleys, or penetrations. Brick quantities are reported as coverage area, not individual bricks. Pricing inputs are user-supplied material allowances only; no labor, tax, freight, foundation, framing, MEP, or permits are included.
